Website guide

Explore the courses

Browse 20 cooking and baking courses. Search by name or use the filters to narrow your selection.

Try a sign-up

Choose a course and enter a name, email address and eight-digit Singapore mobile number starting with 8 or 9. Invalid entries show a helpful message. Check your details before continuing.

Ask the course assistant

Ask about prices or nut allergies. Answers use course documents, with source links available beside supported responses.

Workshop invitation

The Pastries Workshop & Treat invitation appears after 10 seconds without interaction. Use “Workshop invite” to open it again.
